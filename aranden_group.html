Who We Are

A Diversified
Enterprise at Work

Aranden Capital (Private) Limited, trading as Aranden Group, is a diversified Zimbabwean enterprise operating across multiple sectors. We bridge the gap between local demand and global supply markets, delivering integrated sourcing, procurement advisory, and supply chain management services.

Our clients span mining, agriculture, transport, construction, government, and development sectors — and we serve them all with the same commitment to compliance, transparency, and operational excellence.

"Strong compliance, transparency, and operational excellence in every transaction."
